How It Works

1. Sign Up

Create your account as a donor or hospital staff. Fill in basic details and blood group.

🩸

Register your blood donation. The system updates inventory in real time.

🏥

3. Request

Hospitals raise blood requests during emergencies. Admins manage fulfillment.

✅

4. Fulfil

Admin approves requests, deducts inventory, and notifies the requester.
